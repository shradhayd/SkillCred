Information We Collect

We collect information you provide when creating an account, completing your profile, booking sessions, or communicating with other users:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password.
  • Profile Information: Skills, bio, and profile photo.
  • Transaction Data: Credits earned and spent, session history.
  • Communications: Messages exchanged through the platform.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, and interactions.
  • Device Information: Browser type, OS, and IP address.

Sharing Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share data only in these circumstances:

  • With Other Users: Your public profile is visible to other platform members.
  • Service Providers: Trusted vendors who help operate our platform, bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or government authority.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.
✅

We will never sell your personal data to advertisers or data brokers.

Data Retention

  • Account data retained for the lifetime of your account plus 90 days after deletion.

07

Security

We implement TLS/SSL encryption, bcrypt password hashing, regular security audits, access controls, and automated monitoring for suspicious activity. While we take every reasonable precaution, no internet transmission is 100% secure — please use a strong, unique password.

08

Children's Privacy

SkillCred is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, contact us immediately.

09

Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you via email or a prominent notice on the platform. Continued use of SkillCred after changes constitutes your acceptance.
